# Analysis plan: March 2026 price increase and retention

Quirks that apply: monthly price change (March 2026, $10 to $12), free plans never end, future end dates, future start dates, September 2025 spike, timestamps everywhere, trials mature late. Corrections that apply: all three (tenure and start cohort splits; stable recording before choosing a baseline; brief leads with a recommendation).

- **Headline: monthly-plan churn before and after.** Churn is the share of monthly subscribers active at the start of a calendar month whose subscription ends in that month (subscriber-months, compared on dates). After: March to August 2026 (six closed months; September 2026 is partial and excluded). Baseline: October 2025 to February 2026, the months in which end dates are recorded the same way (checked in the script with `stable_over_time` on whether the end date carries a time of day and on days from last payment to end). Each headline carries its comparison in percentage points and relative percent, raw and at the same tenure (after-period rates reweighted to the baseline tenure mix). A same-season baseline (March to August 2025) is shown as a sensitivity only, labelled as recorded differently. Annual plan (price unchanged at $100) gets the same churn measure as a comparison group.
- **Quirks handled:** free plans excluded; starts after 2026-09-24 excluded; future end dates count as active; dates compared as dates, not timestamps; monthly subscriptions that never made a payment are counted and excluded as not paying; the September 2025 cohort is named wherever it sits in a comparison and a sensitivity drops it; trials only enter as a source of new subscribers (from August 2026, after the window).
- **Segments:** tenure band (0-2, 3-5, 6-11, 12+ months at the start of the month), start cohort (existing: started before 2026-03-01, saw their price rise; new: started on or after, only saw $12), country, device type, and main payment method (card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, a proxy for billing platform). For each group: baseline and after churn, change in pp, tenure-adjusted change, a 95% interval, and whether its change differs from the rest of the base beyond noise. A group "stands out" only if its tenure-adjusted rise is clearly larger or smaller than the rest's.
- **Money at stake:** over the six observed months, the extra revenue from the $2 rise against the revenue lost to extra churn (excess churners at the same tenure, valued at the old $10 price), for all monthly subscribers and per group. Next to it, a 12-month projection (September 2026 to August 2027) for the monthly base active on 2026-09-01: revenue at $12 with after-period churn against revenue at $10 with baseline churn, for the whole base and per group, which is the yearly cost or gain of a rollback for that group.
- **Has the rate held:** churn by month since the change among subscribers present before March 2026, by tenure band, saved as a table, and labelled steady, fading or still rising; the projection is shown next to the observed-horizon answer.
- **Is the forward group the same people:** new monthly starts by month with their source (trial or not), country and device mix, saved as a table (a jump in July 2026 starts and trial-fed starts from August 2026 are already visible in the profile).
- **Checks in code:** `date_boundaries` on start, end and payment dates; `matured_only` for months closed by 2026-09-24; `join_fan_out` for users and payments onto subscriptions; `parts_sum_to_total` for segment subscriber-months and churners against the totals; `stable_over_time` on end-date recording.
